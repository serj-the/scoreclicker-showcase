## On the court

ScoreClicker keeps track of points, games and sets, with a large colour-coded scoreboard and spoken score announcements. The watch puts scoring on your wrist; the phone provides a larger match view and settings.

## What it does

- Tracks tennis points, games, sets and tie-breaks.
- Supports singles and doubles, configurable match rules, and pickleball scoring.
- Announces scores and supports undoing a point.
- Synchronises match state between iPhone and Apple Watch.
- Includes watch-side match storage and deferred result transfer when the phone reconnects.
- Provides match settings, results and a player queue on iPhone.
- Offers English, Russian and Vietnamese interface options.

## Designed around the score

Green and orange distinguish the two sides. Large numerals keep the score prominent, while animated light fields and transitions give the interface its visual character.

The custom intro was created using a Fraunhofer diffraction simulation — bringing procedural graphics into a small, everyday app.
